FAQ

Good questions.

How do I start a project?

Email us with what you have in mind, or order through our Fiverr profile. We reply with a plan, a timeline and a fixed quote before anything begins.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. You get an agreed delivery date up front, before any work starts.

Are revisions included?

Yes. Every project includes revisions, and the exact number is confirmed in your quote.

Do I own the final work?

Yes. On completion you receive the files and full rights to use them for your business or channel.

Can you put it live for me?

Yes. We can publish videos to your channel, launch websites on your domain and set up bots on your Discord server.
